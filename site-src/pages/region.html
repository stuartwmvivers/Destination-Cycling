How these figures were made

Source. Daily weather for {{YEARS}} from Open-Meteo's historical archive, which serves ECMWF's model analysis of past weather on a grid of about 9 km. It is built from real observations (stations, satellites, buoys) but it is not a single thermometer reading. 2016 is left out because only a coarser 25 km grid covers that year, which blurs neighbouring towns together.

Averages. "{{YEARS}} average" is the mean of the {{N_YEARS}} years; the shaded band in the seasonal chart runs from the lowest to the highest year. Whole-year temperatures are the mean of the twelve monthly values; day counts and rainfall are totals.
