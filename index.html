Destination Cycling

Where in Europe could you keep a base for road cycling all year round? This site compares candidate places by how much good riding their weather allows, month by month, against two UK baselines, and is open about how far to trust each figure.

What's coming

Weather is only part of what makes a good cycling base. The plan is a combined score that ranks places on:

  • ClimbingClimbs within riding distance, measured from terrain data. The most important factor.
  • Weather at the climbsRideable days at the height of the climbs, not just in the town, weighted to autumn through spring.
  • Quiet roadsMinor paved roads that join up into loops, from OpenStreetMap.
  • Getting thereDrive time to an airport with year-round direct flights from Scotland.
